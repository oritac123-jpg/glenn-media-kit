Media Profile

Glenn Cohen is a former Mossad officer and Chief Operational Psychologist, former Israeli Air Force pilot, hostage negotiator, and internationally recognized expert in resilience, leadership, and performance under extreme conditions. Following October 7, he led the psychological team responsible for debriefing 168 hostages upon their release from Hamas captivity.

Featured in leading international media, podcasts, and forums, Glenn brings a rare firsthand perspective on survival, hope, and human capability. Simon & Schuster recently acquired the rights to his upcoming book on post-traumatic growth.

Why Interview Glenn?

Glenn brings a perspective few others can: three decades inside the Mossad, the Israeli Air Force, and high-stakes hostage negotiation, and the responsibility of gently escorting the hostages on their journey home.

His interviews challenge conventional thinking about trauma, resilience, hope, leadership, and what human beings are truly capable of.

Glenn truly changed my way of thinking about the hostage situation and, more fundamentally, about Israeli society.

Michael Oren
Former Israeli Ambassador to the United States · New York Times bestselling author
