Why Glenn

Short · 50 words

Glenn Cohen is a former Mossad Chief Operational Psychologist and hostage negotiator. For 30 years he selected, prepared and debriefed elite operatives. After October 7th he founded and led the team that debriefed the 168 returned hostages. Today he speaks worldwide on resilience, leadership and performance under pressure.
