Keynotes

Keynote 01

From Captivity to Resilience

What 168 returned hostages teach us about surviving, and growing, under extreme pressure.

Read more

Glenn was the first mental health professional to meet the hostages on their return, wrote the protocol for receiving them, and led the team that debriefed all 168. He shares the coping strategies that carried them through: small wins, finding control, togetherness and meaning, and turns them into tools anyone can use in their own crisis.

45–60 min + Q&AKeynote or fireside chat
Keynote 02

Crack the ELITE Code

How elite units select, prepare and debrief, and what your leaders can use the next morning.

Read more

The ELITE Method (Execution, Learning, Integrity, Toughness, Emotion) was developed over decades in the Mossad and the elite units. At its heart is the E Factor, the emotional engine of trust, courage and execution under pressure. Teams leave with practical tools to perform when it matters most.
