Keynotes

Keynote 01

From Captivity to Resilience

Behind the scenes with the Hostage Debrief Team Leader

After October 7th, Glenn served more than five months of emergency reserve duty in the IDF Hostage Negotiation Unit. He was the first mental health professional to meet the hostages on their return, wrote the protocol for receiving them, and founded and led the team that debriefed all 168 returned hostages.

Glenn shares what they endured, and the coping strategies that carried them through: small wins, finding control where there seems to be none, togetherness, and meaning. He takes the audience behind the scenes as a Mossad psychologist, hostage negotiator and POW-survival trainer, and turns these lessons into tools anyone can use in their own crisis.

  • Why some people break and others grow under extreme pressure
  • Small wins: regaining control when almost nothing is in your control
  • Why togetherness is our oxygen, in captivity and in teams
45–60 min + Q&AKeynote or fireside chatCorporate & community

Keynote 02

Crack the ELITE Code of Peak Performance and Resilience

What leaders can learn from how elite operatives are selected, prepared and debriefed

The ELITE Method is a leadership framework Glenn developed over decades in the Mossad, the elite units and high-stakes psychological operations. At its heart is the E Factor: the emotional engine that drives trust, resilience, courage, adaptability and execution under pressure.

Glenn reveals how elite performers are trained to think creatively in uncertainty, lead through complexity, and approach the toughest challenges believing there is no such thing as “mission impossible.” Leaders and teams leave with practical tools to perform in the moments that matter most.

  • The five ELITE keys: Execution, Learning, Integrity, Toughness, Emotion
  • Debrief culture: how elite teams learn faster than everyone else
  • Performing under uncertainty without burning out
45–90 minKeynote or interactive workshopLeadership teams & conferences

Full · 150 words

Glenn Cohen is a former Mossad Chief Operational Psychologist and hostage negotiator. Born and raised in New York, he served as an Air Force pilot before spending three decades selecting, preparing and debriefing elite operatives, retiring from the Mossad with the equivalent rank of colonel.

After October 7th he served more than five months of emergency reserve duty in the IDF Hostage Negotiation Unit. He was the first mental health professional to meet the hostages on their return, wrote the protocol for receiving them, and founded and led the team that debriefed all 168 returned hostages. He has briefed the President and the Prime Minister on the lessons of captivity.

Glenn developed the ELITE Method for peak performance and resilience, and speaks to corporations, leadership teams and communities worldwide. Simon & Schuster has acquired his upcoming book on post-traumatic growth.
